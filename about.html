What we actually do

Books 'n' Much More takes the world's most powerful books — on mindfulness, discipline, purpose, relationships, and inner growth — and breaks them into short, bilingual lessons in Hindi and English. Each lesson is written to be read in minutes, understood without effort, and — most importantly — acted on the same day.

Our Vision

To make life-changing wisdom accessible to every Hindi and English speaker — one lesson, one action, one day at a time.

What makes us different

Every lesson comes with a real-life action challenge, not just an idea to admire. Bookmark what resonates, track what you've read, and build your own library of lessons that actually changed something for you. We didn't want to build another place to read about wisdom — we wanted to build a place where wisdom gets used.

Why bilingual, why us

Growth shouldn't depend on which language you think in. Whether you reach for Hindi or English, the same lesson — the same depth — is there for you. From ancient texts like the Bhagavad Gita to modern voices like Sadhguru, our library keeps growing — every book rewritten, re-thought, and made ready for real life.
